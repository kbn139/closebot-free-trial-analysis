## What 100 messages a month really covers

A "message" here is one AI reply, and the free plan caps you at 100 a month. That number is more generous than it sounds and less generous than you'd hope, depending on how you build.

A well-built agent splits a single thought across several short messages — the way a good human setter texts. Ask about availability and you might get three replies back to back: a confirmation, a time window, and a question. So 100 messages is maybe 25 to 35 real conversations if your agent is chatty, and closer to 50 if it's terse.

That's enough to answer the only questions that matter before you pay:

- Does it sound like a person or like a bot reading a script?
- Does it ask for the information you actually need, or wander?
- When a lead goes sideways, does it recover or plow ahead?
- Does it book on your calendar without breaking?

It's not enough to run a live campaign. If you're putting the agent in front of real traffic, you'll burn 100 messages in a few days. The free plan is a workshop, not a launchpad.

There's an escape hatch if you're mid-test and hit the wall: on the free plan you can pay as you go at $0.08 per message beyond the 100-message ceiling. That's a per-message top-up, not a subscription.

**What's locked on free and unlocked on paid:** the larger template library, additional user seats, extra storage, and extra agents. Free is one agent, one seat, 1 MB. If your test requires a second person looking at the account, you're already past free.

## The costs that aren't on the pricing card

Two line items catch people out.

**The CRM underneath.** CloseBot is CRM-native. It integrates with HighLevel, HubSpot, and custom CRMs, and it takes over the text conversations already flowing through them. If you don't run a CRM, the subscription isn't your whole bill — you're adding one. GoHighLevel's own entry tier starts around $97 a month, which is a real cost on top of CloseBot. A business targeting 1,000 AI messages a month is realistically looking at something near $180 a month total, not $84.

**Storage and seats.** The business plans include 1 MB of knowledge storage, and 1 MB of text is roughly 1,000 pages. If you're feeding the agent big PDFs, that goes quickly. Additional storage runs from $0.10 to $3.00 per MB per month depending on volume, and extra user seats are $5 each. On the Agency track, storage is billed per MB per day and can be marked up to clients.

There's also a billing subtlety worth knowing: one message equals one segment normally, but if you unlock the Agent Node's "unlimited potential" — stacking many tools and unlimited instruction size — billing switches to token costs, and a single message can consume several segments. Heavy, tool-rich agents cost more per reply than the message count suggests. Budget conservatively if you plan to build something elaborate.

## What the free plan can't tell you

Three things only show up at scale or on paid plans.

**Multi-channel behavior under real traffic.** A free plan with 100 messages can't stress-test an agent across SMS, web chat, and email simultaneously. The failure modes — a dropped handoff, a booking retry that loops, a lead who replies at 2 a.m. — surface on volume.

**Personas across clients.** In CloseBot V2, personas are independent of the bot, so one tone definition can be reused across different client accounts with variables. That's an agency feature, and it's locked behind the paid tiers.

**White labeling and rebilling.** If your plan is to sell AI setting under your own brand, the free plan shows you nothing about the part you'd actually charge for. CloseBot's FAQ notes directly that the business plan doesn't give you the re-billing and white-labeling view that the Agency plan offers — even during the 7-day Agency trial.

## The setup question nobody asks until they're inside

CloseBot does not connect to Instagram or WhatsApp itself. It answers the text-based channels inside your CRM. If Instagram is wired into your GoHighLevel Conversations inbox, the agent replies to those DMs. If you don't run a CRM at all, the "standalone" path still means adopting a system to carry conversations before CloseBot has anything to answer.

For agencies already living in HighLevel, this is a feature: one brain across many client accounts. For a solo coach whose entire pipeline is Instagram DMs and who doesn't run a CRM, it's an extra subscription and an extra setup project layered on top of the thing you actually wanted.

Answer this honestly before paying: **where do my leads actually talk to me?** Forms, SMS, and a CRM inbox favor CloseBot. Instagram DMs with no CRM behind them favor a DM-native tool instead.

## A realistic plan for your first week

The free plan and the 7-day trial are two different experiments, and running them in that order saves you both time and money.

1. **Week one, free plan.** Build one agent from a template. Point it at a knowledge base that mirrors what you'd actually feed it. Run test conversations yourself, including an annoying one — off-topic questions, a request for a discount, a lead who goes quiet.
2. **Check the message burn.** Count how many replies a single realistic conversation consumed. Multiply by your actual monthly lead volume. That's your plan tier, and it's usually higher than people expect.
3. **Then start the 7-day trial** on the tier you calculated. Not the base Core tier because it's cheapest — the tier that matches your volume, so you're testing the thing you'd buy.
4. **Watch one specific number during the trial.** Whether your limits hold. Plans are month to month, so downgrading isn't painful, but the storage and agent add-ons you switch on can quietly outlive their usefulness.

## Questions that come up before starting

**Do I need a credit card for the free plan or trial?** No. CloseBot's plans page states plainly that you start for free with no credit card and no lock-in.

**What happens when I hit 100 messages on the free plan?** You can either stop, or pay as you go at $0.08 per message over the cap. CloseBot's own documentation suggests that if you're going over regularly, a paid plan is the cheaper route.

**Can I cancel mid-trial?** Yes. Plans run month to month and can be upgraded, downgraded, or cancelled at any time. Just remember there are no refunds after billing starts, so cancel before the 7 days are up if it isn't a fit.

**Is the free plan actually the real product?** It's the real builder with hard ceilings — one agent, one seat, 1 MB, 100 messages. You can build and test a genuine agent. You can't run a campaign or support a second user.

**Does it work in languages other than English?** CloseBot states support for 40+ languages and up, tied to the underlying models — if Claude and ChatGPT work in your language, the agent should too.

**How long until an agent is live?** CloseBot's position is minutes to hours, not weeks, using templates and the testing portal. The constraint on that timeline is your knowledge base, not the builder.
